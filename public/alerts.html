Alerts

A notification on this device when something you care about lands on our shelf. No email, no app. We keep only your browser's push address and what you ticked.

Arrival alerts come once a day around 10 am Atlantic, only on days with arrivals. The market pulse comes Monday mornings.
On iPhone or iPad

Safari only delivers alerts to a page saved to your Home Screen. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, open Exor Alerts from there and turn alerts on.
